THE NEXORA PROCESS

One managed process. Clear responsibility at every step.

NEXORA coordinates the relationship between your business and the creative team. You communicate your needs through NEXORA; we manage planning, assignment, review and delivery.

01

Apply and introduce your business

Submit a client application with your business details, goals, current brand materials and the kind of creative support you need. NEXORA reviews applications before granting client workspace access.

02

Discuss and define the work

For a specific request, NEXORA clarifies the objective, audience, deliverables, formats, timeline and other requirements. A quotation and terms are presented for agreement before production starts.

03

Assign the creative work

NEXORA selects an internal designer based on the project's requirements and availability. The designer receives an approved brief and only the client information needed for the assignment.

04

Produce and review

The designer develops the work and submits it for internal quality review. NEXORA checks alignment with the agreed brief before presenting a client-facing draft.

05

Review and refine

You review the submitted design and provide consolidated feedback. Revisions are handled according to the agreed scope and revision terms. Changes that alter the original brief may require a revised quotation.

06

Approve and receive final files

After approval and completion of the agreed terms, NEXORA prepares the final deliverables in the specified formats and records the project as completed.

What this means for clients

You do not need to search for designers, compare bids or coordinate production separately. NEXORA remains responsible for managing the creative workflow and communicating project progress.
