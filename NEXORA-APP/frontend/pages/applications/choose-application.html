How would you like to work with NEXORA?

Choose the application that matches your purpose. Each application collects information relevant to the relationship you are proposing.

FOR BUSINESSES

Apply as a client

Introduce your business, products or services, audience, goals and creative requirements. This helps NEXORA understand your needs and determine whether its services are appropriate.

  • Business and contact information
  • Current brand identity and marketing materials
  • Creative needs, goals and readiness
  • Expected timing and budget range

FOR CREATIVE PROFESSIONALS

Apply as a designer

Present your design capabilities, experience, portfolio, tools, availability and approach to professional collaboration.

  • Professional and contact information
  • Specialties and software proficiency
  • Portfolio and relevant experience
  • Availability and working expectations
Continue as a designer

What happens after submission?

Applications are reviewed by NEXORA. Submission does not automatically create an approved client or designer account. An administrator may request additional information, approve the application or decline it. After submission, you receive a reference number. NEXORA reviews each application and may request clarification before making a decision.
